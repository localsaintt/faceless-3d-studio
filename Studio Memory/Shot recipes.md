# Shot recipes

Proven camera setups (beach kit: waterline y≈0, sea +Y; street kit: road along X). Reuse the *pattern*, never the exact numbers: every video needs new camera paths (the QC originality check compares them).

| Recipe | Use for | Pattern | Worked in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Offshore diorama push | Opening establishing shot | Camera 10 m up, ~25 m offshore, pushing 5 m toward a landmark; f/0.04 | Video 1 s01 (proto) |
| Aerial crane | The moment the rule changes; counters | Camera 26→40 m up above the crowd, looking slightly inland; f/0.06 | Video 1 s02 |
| Elevated side-on story shot | Two characters far apart (cause → effect) | Camera 6 m up, 10 m to the side and behind, both in frame; labels on both | Video 1 s03 |
| Road-edge pan follow | One fast object passing (boom moment) | Static camera at the road edge ahead of the object; target follows it; boom when the cone passes the lens | Video 1 s04 |
| Street crane | Many objects at once (escalation) | Camera 9→13 m up at the beach side, looking down the street | Video 1 s05 |
| Low crane-up over crowd | "Everyone is…" beats | Starts 2.4 m high at the crowd edge, rises to 13 m | Video 1 s06 |
| Slow orbit | Aftermath / awe beat | Radius 13 m, 3.5 m high, 50° arc around the landmark | Video 1 s10 |
| Close two-shot push | Punchline dialogue | 1.6 m high, 5–8 m away, f/0.7 | Video 1 s11 |
