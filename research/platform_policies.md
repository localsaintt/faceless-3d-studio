## 1. TikTok

### 1.1 Creator Rewards Program (CRP) eligibility

| Requirement | Detail | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Age | 18+ | VERIFIED (official): TikTok FAQ https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7581821550694013452 ; Creator Academy https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/creator-rewards-program |
| Followers | at least 10,000 | VERIFIED (official), same pages |
| Views | at least 100,000 video views in the last 30 days | VERIFIED (official), same pages |
| Account type | **Personal account only.** Business accounts and political/government accounts are not eligible. Authentic account info (real name, DOB) required | VERIFIED (official), FAQ and Creator Academy |
| Standing | Good standing; no unusual or suspicious activity (artificial engagement, manipulating the rewards system) | VERIFIED (official): https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/monetization-creativity-program-video-eligible |
| Countries | Creator Academy lists US, UK, Germany, Japan, South Korea, France, Mexico, Brazil. Third-party lists that add Canada, Australia, Italy, Spain, SE Asia conflict with each other | Core list: VERIFIED (official), Creator Academy. Extra countries: UNVERIFIED |

### 1.2 Video-level eligibility

- **Length:** The FAQ says "at least one minute". Creator Academy and Newsroom say "over a minute" or "longer than 1 minute". The wording differs, so **target 61 seconds or more** to be safe. VERIFIED (official): FAQ above; Newsroom https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program?lang=en
- The video must be posted **after** joining CRP. VERIFIED (official), FAQ.
- Each video needs **at least 1,000 qualified For You feed views**. VERIFIED (official), FAQ and Creator Academy.
- **Excluded formats:** ads, paid promotions and branded content; Duets; Stitches; Photo Mode videos. Split-screen and slide-style videos are treated as low quality. VERIFIED (official), Creator Academy.
- The content must be "filmed, designed, and produced entirely by yourself" (FAQ). The EEA terms add "and/or add new ideas to preexisting content". VERIFIED (official): FAQ; EEA terms https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-eea/en

### 1.3 Originality and quality

- CRP pays for "high-quality, original content". Originality means content that is unique to the creator and shows their point of view or creative process. VERIFIED (official), Newsroom.
- **Unoriginal** (TikTok Originality Policy) includes: content copied wholesale; content repurposed without creative edits; mash-ups of several sources; visible watermarks or other people's logos. Unoriginal content is ineligible for CRP, and originality is a key metric in the rewards formula. VERIFIED (official): https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/tiktok-originality-policy
- The FAQ's examples of non-original content: Duets and Stitches, and content "copied completely from others and with others' watermarks". Accounts are periodically reviewed. VERIFIED (official), FAQ.
- TikTok Shop seller guidance goes further. It says: don't re-upload the same video; minor overlays, filters or audio swaps don't make content original; and AI output that is near-identical to other creators' work (same tool, prompt, avatar or template) can count as unoriginal. VERIFIED (official) **for TikTok Shop only**: https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7841352058554114 . Applying it to CRP is UNVERIFIED (my inference), but it is a reasonable signal of how TikTok thinks about AI templating.

### 1.4 Flag and violation system: the playbook's "five flags in 30 days" claim

- Creator Academy: "if your account has **5 or more ineligible videos in the past 30 days**, it **may** be disqualified." VERIFIED (official): https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/monetization-creativity-program-video-eligible
  - **Verdict: largely confirmed, with corrections.** The trigger is any 5 *ineligible* videos, not only non-original or low-quality ones, and removal is discretionary ("may"), not automatic.
- The claim that "5 account violations in total = permanently ineligible" appears only in a third-party summary. UNVERIFIED.
- **Appeals:** the FAQ says all flags and disqualifications can be appealed **within 30 days** of notice, review takes about 3 days, and video appeals don't affect the account. Creator Academy says **80 days** for a video and **30 days** for an account disqualification, with reviews of up to 14 days. The official pages conflict, so **plan on 30 days**. VERIFIED (official), both pages.
- Reapplying 30 days after rejection is a creator report. UNVERIFIED.

### 1.5 Qualified views, Additional Reward and RPM

- **Qualified views:** unique For You feed views with at least 5 seconds of watch time (search views reportedly need 30 seconds). Fraudulent, paid, promoted and artificial views are excluded, and repeat views from one account count once. VERIFIED (official): https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-rewards-program/how-rewards-work ; FAQ; Creator Academy.
- **Total reward = Standard Reward + Additional Reward.** The Additional Reward was introduced in spring 2025 to favour high-quality content. VERIFIED (official), Creator Academy.
- **RPM factors:** originality, play duration/watch time, search value, audience engagement, viewer location. TikTok publishes no fixed RPM. VERIFIED (official), Newsroom and support "how rewards work".
- Typical payouts of roughly $0.40 to $1.00 per 1,000 qualified views. UNVERIFIED (third-party blogs).

### 1.6 AI-generated content (AIGC)

- Creators must label **realistic** AI-generated or edited content that depicts people or scenes. TikTok provides a creator AIGC label toggle. VERIFIED (official): https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/new-labels-for-disclosing-ai-generated-content ; https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/supporting-responsible-transparent-ai-generated-content
- TikTok **auto-labels** content made with its own AI effects, and content carrying **C2PA Content Credentials** from other tools. It also adds invisible watermarks to AIGC. VERIFIED (official): https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/partnering-with-our-industry-to-advance-ai-transparency-and-literacy ; https://newsroom.tiktok.com/more-ways-to-spot-shape-and-understand-ai-content?lang=en
  - Practical effect: if your pipeline exports files with Content Credentials (some AI image/video tools embed them), TikTok will label the video automatically.
- Unlabeled content "may be removed, restricted, or labeled by our team", depending on potential harm. Labeling does not excuse harmful fakes such as fake authoritative sources, crisis events, or public-figure endorsements. VERIFIED (official): https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity
- The current Community Guidelines were released 2026-08-25 and took effect 2026-09-24. VERIFIED (official): https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines
- I found no official statement that the AIGC label reduces CRP eligibility or reach. Stylized 3D animation is not "realistic", but labeling it costs little. UNVERIFIED (inference).

### 1.7 Duplicate accounts and impersonation

- Multiple accounts are allowed (for example for fan content or creative expression), but not to deceive or break rules. Impersonation is prohibited, and a new account may not be used to get around a ban or restriction. VERIFIED (official): Integrity & Authenticity page above.
- CRP terms exclude metrics generated by the creator or by "accounts acting in connection with Creator" to inflate metrics. VERIFIED (official): https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/creator-rewards-program-us/en
- The claim that "each account must meet eligibility individually" or that "one ID = one CRP account" comes from creator posts. UNVERIFIED.

### 1.8 Music

- The **Commercial Music Library (CML)** is pre-cleared for commercial use on TikTok only. Some "Premium" tracks may carry a fee. VERIFIED (official): https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/commercial-music-library-user-terms/en
- **General Music Library** songs "are not pre-cleared for commercial content" and are not available to brands or businesses. Business accounts must use CML or clear rights themselves. VERIFIED (official): https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/commercial-use-of-music-on-tiktok ; https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/audio-library-royalty-free-music
- TikTok One (branded creator marketplace) requires CML-only music. VERIFIED (official): https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-one-creative-suite-creator-commercial-terms
- For **organic CRP videos on a personal account**, I found no official rule requiring CML. CRP videos must comply with the Copyright Policy. The claim that copyrighted music disqualifies a video is a creator anecdote, UNVERIFIED. Safest choice: original or AI-generated music you own the rights to, or CML tracks, especially if any brand deals are planned.

### 1.9 Posting via API or automation

- The **Content Posting API (Direct Post)** is official. Unaudited clients can post only as SELF_ONLY (private), with caps around 5 users per 24 hours and about 15 posts per day per creator account. Public posting requires a TikTok audit. A tool that "only uploads to accounts you or your team manages" is listed as an unacceptable use case for the developer program. VERIFIED (official): https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/content-sharing-guidelines ; https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/content-posting-api-reference-direct-post
- Scheduling through audited partner tools (Adobe, social-media managers) is officially supported. VERIFIED (official): https://newsroom.tiktok.com/direct-post?lang=en
- I found no official statement that API-posted videos are ineligible for CRP. UNVERIFIED either way.

## 2. YouTube

### 2.1 YPP eligibility (current, as of 2026-11-02)

- **Full YPP (ad revenue):** 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 qualified public watch hours in 12 months, or **10 million qualified public Shorts views in 90 days**. Shorts watch time does not count toward the 4,000 hours. Every channel also gets a manual review, needs no active Community Guidelines strikes, and must have 2-Step Verification on. VERIFIED (official): https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851 ; https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-monetization-qualified-watch-hours-shorts-views/
- **Early tier (fan funding and Shopping, no ad revenue):** 500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in 90 days, and either 3,000 watch hours in 12 months or 3 million Shorts views in 90 days. VERIFIED (official): https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240
- **UPCOMING CHANGE:** from **2027-02-01**, new applicants need 1,000 subscribers plus either **8,000** watch hours in 365 days or **20 million** Shorts views in 90 days. Existing partners keep their status. Secondary sources also describe a separate 10M-per-90-day Shorts bar to keep Shorts revenue sharing, and an "active channel" rule with a 90-day recovery window. Core numbers: VERIFIED (official): https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009 and https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/ . Retention-bar details: VERIFIED (secondary) only (metricool, nofilmschool). **A channel that qualifies before 2027-02-01 gets in at today's thresholds.**

### 2.2 Inauthentic content (renamed from "repetitious content", July 15 2025)

- The rename took effect July 15, 2025. YouTube says it clarifies existing rules and covers **repetitive or mass-produced** content. VERIFIED (official): https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392
- Examples YouTube gives: image slideshows, templated storylines, or scrolling text with minimal narrative, commentary or educational value; "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production without adding the creator's original, authentic insights or perspective"; and repetitive, emotionally manipulative series. VERIFIED (official), same page as surfaced in search.
- The YPP FAQ says AI use is not disqualifying in itself, but channels are still subject to rejection or suspension if the content is not clearly original. VERIFIED (official): https://support.google.com/youtube/community-guide/271248162
- The **Spam policy** (separate, Community Guidelines) prohibits using automated tools or AI to churn out high volumes of similar content with minimal changes. Its example: channels with "the exact same background music and repetitive AI generated imagery across many videos, with each video reading out an AI-generated script." Posting "a few variations of a video" is OK. VERIFIED (official): https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973

### 2.3 Reused content

- The reused content policy was **unchanged** by the July 2025 update. It covers other people's content (clips, compilations, reactions) with no significant original commentary or modification, including content "uploaded many times by other creators". It applies to the **channel as a whole**: monetization can be removed channel-wide, and enforcement can extend to the owner's other channels. Creating new channels to evade a suspension can get all channels terminated. VERIFIED (official): https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392

### 2.4 Shorts revenue sharing

- Ads shown between Shorts in the Shorts Feed are pooled each month. Each Short's share is split for music: no music means 100% to the Creator Pool; 1 track means half; 2 tracks means one third. Each creator gets a share of the pool equal to their share of engaged views, and **keeps 45%** of that allocation, music or not. Worked example: $100k pool, 1M of 100M views, $405 to the creator. VERIFIED (official): https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220
  - **Practical point for this pipeline: original or no-licensed-music Shorts keep the full pool allocation.**
- Shorts can be up to 3 minutes. A Short **over 1 minute with an active Content ID claim is blocked globally** and cannot be monetized. VERIFIED (official): https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877 ; https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13486873
- Starting 2027-02-01, ads targeted to five or fewer channels are reported to pay a direct 45% share on top of the pool. VERIFIED (official): https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/ (re-check before relying on it).

### 2.5 Altered or synthetic content disclosure

- Creators must tick the YouTube Studio "Altered content" / AI use question (under Attributes at upload) when GenAI realistically: makes a real person appear to say or do something they didn't; alters footage of a real event or place; generates a realistic scene that didn't occur; or produces music that is the main focus of the video. VERIFIED (official): https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491
- **Exempt:** clearly unrealistic content (YouTube's examples include a unicorn in a fantasy world and "AI-animated content in a fully animated video"); minor aesthetic edits such as beauty, colour and lighting filters, background blur or vintage effects; and productivity uses such as AI-written scripts, ideas or captions. VERIFIED (official), same page and https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/disclosing-ai-generated-content/
- Disclosure does not reduce reach or monetization. YouTube may add a label itself, and may enforce against creators who repeatedly fail to disclose. VERIFIED (official), same page.
- **Implication for stylized 3D animation:** generally exempt. Disclose anyway if a video realistically depicts a real place, such as a photoreal Miami Beach event that didn't happen, or uses AI music as the featured element. Inference based on the VERIFIED rules.

### 2.6 Content ID and music

- **YouTube Audio Library:** royalty-free, no Content ID claims; safe for Shorts and long-form. VERIFIED (official): https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15577610
- **Shorts audio library** tracks are licensed for Shorts but create an invisible, non-disputable Content ID claim (the track costs pool share as described in 2.4). VERIFIED (official): https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13053317
- **Creator Music:** licensing or revenue sharing works on long-form only, not on Shorts. VERIFIED (official): https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11610609
- Manual claims on very short or unintentional music uses can no longer monetize. Automated Content ID match claims are unaffected. VERIFIED (official): https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/updates-to-manual-claiming-policies/
- "Free" music from third-party sites is often registered in Content ID, which can produce claims. UNVERIFIED (common creator experience).

### 2.7 API posting

- The YouTube Data API `videos.insert` is official. A 2020 notice locked uploads from unverified projects (created after 2020-07-28) to private until the project passed an audit. The current reference page reportedly says unverified projects are no longer restricted to private; the two pages conflict, so test with your project. Videos locked private this way can't be appealed and must be re-uploaded. VERIFIED (official): https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/videos/insert ; https://developers.google.com/youtube/data/revision_history ; https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7300965

## 3. Meta (Facebook and Instagram)

### 3.1 Facebook unoriginal-content crackdown (2025 to 2026)

- On 2025-07-14 Meta announced that accounts which repeatedly reuse other people's videos, photos or text **lose monetization access for a period** and get **reduced distribution on everything they post**. Detected duplicate videos get reduced distribution, and Meta is testing links that attribute copies to the original. Meaningful enhancement is required: "simply stitching together clips or adding your watermark does not qualify". VERIFIED (official): https://creators.facebook.com/blog/combating-unoriginal-content (Meta newsroom post of 2025-07-14)
- Scale: in the first half of 2025 Meta acted on about 500k spammy or fake-engagement accounts and removed about 10M impersonator profiles. VERIFIED (official), same post.
- On 2026-03 Meta reported that views and watch time on original Reels roughly doubled in the second half of 2025, and that it removed more than 20M impersonating accounts in 2025. It clarified that reacting, stitching or narrating existing footage without adding anything meaningful will likely be deprioritized. VERIFIED (official): https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/
- Related pages: https://about.fb.com/news/2025/04/cracking-down-spammy-content-facebook/ (April 2025 spam crackdown); https://en-gb.facebook.com/business/help/3382366608650437 (limited-originality violations).

### 3.2 Instagram originality ranking

- Instagram's help center says it prioritizes original content and is **less likely to recommend**: reposts of a reel already on Instagram; content with noticeable watermarks; and accounts that regularly collect and reshare others' content. Its ranking explainer adds low-resolution, muted, bordered, mostly-text and already-posted reels to that list. Stitching or watermarking does not count as material editing. VERIFIED (official): https://help.instagram.com/1800814370401535/ ; https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained
- In 2024, Instagram began replacing reposts with the original in recommendations, labeling reposts, and removing aggregators (accounts that repost 10 or more times in 30 days) from recommendations. A 2026 expansion reportedly extended this from Reels to photos and carousels. VERIFIED (secondary): TechCrunch, Nasdaq/SocialMediaToday. I could not find the official announcement or confirm the exact dates.
- **Cross-posting implication:** upload clean, watermark-free masters to each platform. A TikTok watermark on an Instagram Reel is officially down-ranked.

### 3.3 Facebook Content Monetization eligibility

- The program is **invite-only** (as of 2026-03). Creators express interest via Professional Dashboard > Monetization > Content Monetization. A professional-mode profile or Page and an eligible country are required, along with compliance with the Partner Monetization and Content Monetization Policies. Creators may only monetize content they created, took part in creating, or that features them. An "established presence" of at least 30 days applies. VERIFIED (official): https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/creator-fast-track-grow-your-audience-earn-money-on-facebook/ ; https://www.facebook.com/business/help/169845596919485 ; https://www.facebook.com/legal/creator_content_monetization_terms
- **Creator Fast Track** (2026): open to creators in the US, CA, UK or AU, aged 18+, with at least 20k followers on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube and at least 30k video views in 60 days. It gives immediate Content Monetization access and guaranteed pay for posting. Not relevant until the channel has an audience elsewhere. VERIFIED (official): https://creators.facebook.com/creator-fast-track
- No public follower or view threshold for Content Monetization was found. UNVERIFIED.

### 3.4 "AI info" labels and disclosure

- People must use Meta's AI-disclosure tool when posting organic content with **photorealistic video or realistic-sounding audio** that was digitally created or altered. Meta "may apply penalties" for failure to disclose. VERIFIED (official): https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/misinformation/
- Meta applies the "AI info" label when it detects industry signals (C2PA, IPTC) or when the poster self-discloses. It may add a more prominent label to content with a high risk of misleading people on matters of public importance. VERIFIED (official): https://transparency.meta.com/governance/tracking-impact/labeling-ai-content/ ; https://about.fb.com/news/2024/04/metas-approach-to-labeling-ai-generated-content-and-manipulated-media/ ; https://about.fb.com/news/2026/02/meta-prepares-for-2026-us-midterms/
- That non-photorealistic or cartoon video with no humans needs no label is UNVERIFIED. It appears only in an academic paper quoting Meta guidance, but it is consistent with the official "photorealistic" qualifier. Stylized 3D animation is likely outside mandatory disclosure, though auto-labels may still appear through C2PA metadata.

### 3.5 Length limits

- **Instagram:** Reels up to 3 minutes are eligible for recommendation to non-followers (from early 2025). Reels can be up to 20 minutes, but those over 3 minutes are not recommended to new audiences. VERIFIED (official): https://creators.instagram.com/blog/the-latest-with-instagram ; https://help.instagram.com/225190788256708
- **Facebook:** on 2025-06-17 Meta announced that all videos would be shared as Reels with "no length or format restrictions", rolling out "in the coming months". VERIFIED (official): https://about.fb.com/news/2025/06/making-it-easier-create-videos-facebook/

## 4. Copyright basics for the pipeline

### 4.1 CC0 and CC-BY assets

- **CC0:** no conditions and no attribution legally required; commercial use allowed. Credit is recommended as good practice. VERIFIED (official): https://wiki.creativecommons.org/wiki/CC0_FAQ
- **CC BY 4.0:** commercial use and adaptation allowed if you give appropriate credit, link the license, and indicate changes. Use **TASL** (Title, Author, Source, License). For video, credit in the description box is acceptable; title is optional for 4.0 but required for 3.0 and earlier. VERIFIED (official): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/deed.en ; https://wiki.creativecommons.org/wiki/Recommended_practices_for_attribution
- **Avoid NC (non-commercial) and ND (no-derivatives) licenses** for monetized videos. Inference from the standard CC license terms.
- CC licenses cover copyright only. Trademark, publicity and privacy rights (for example a recognizable logo or a real person's likeness in a model) are not cleared. VERIFIED (official): https://creativecommons.org/reusing-cc-licensed-content/
- **Practice:** keep an asset ledger (source URL, license, author, date downloaded) and put CC-BY credits in every video description on every platform.

### 4.2 Mixamo

- Characters and animations are royalty-free for personal, commercial and non-profit projects, including films and video. Credit is not required. You may not redistribute the raw character or animation files, for example on asset stores. Mixamo content may not be used to train ML models. It is unavailable for Enterprise/Federated IDs and China-coded accounts. VERIFIED (official): https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html . Redistribution and credit details are VERIFIED (secondary: Adobe community forum answers quoting the FAQ).
- **Rendered video output is fine to monetize.**

### 4.3 AI-generated assets and copyrightability

- The US Copyright Office's *Copyright and AI, Part 2: Copyrightability* (2025-01-29) says AI outputs are protectable only where a human determined sufficient expressive elements: human-authored work perceptible in the output, or creative selection, arrangement or modification. **Prompts alone are not enough.** Using AI as a tool, or including AI material in a larger human work, does not bar protection for the human contributions. No legislative change was recommended. VERIFIED (official): https://www.copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-2-Copyrightability-Report.pdf ; https://www.copyright.gov/newsnet/2025/1060.html
- **Implication:** purely AI-generated elements (a raw AI texture or an unedited AI clip) are likely public domain in the US, so others could reuse them. Your human contributions (scripts, scene composition, edit timing, animation choices) are what protect the work. Keep project files as evidence of authorship. Also check each AI tool's terms of service for commercial-use rights; these vary by tool.

### 4.4 Real place names, landmarks and trademarked buildings

- **Copyright:** 17 U.S.C. §120(a) says copyright in a building does not prevent pictures or "other pictorial representations" of it if it is in or ordinarily visible from a public place. A 3D rendition of a skyline or the Santa Monica Pier structure is very likely fine on copyright. VERIFIED (official statute): https://uscode.house.gov/ (17 USC 120)
- **Trademark:** in *Rock & Roll Hall of Fame v. Gentile Productions*, 134 F.3d 749 (6th Cir. 1998), the court held that a photo of a famous building on a poster did not, without more, infringe the building's claimed trademark. VERIFIED (secondary/legal): https://www.courtlistener.com/opinion/2515490/rock-roll-hall-of-fame-museum-inc-v-gentile-productions/
- **Place names** (Miami Beach, Santa Monica Pier) used descriptively for the setting are generally low risk (nominative or descriptive use). UNVERIFIED (general legal principle; not legal advice).
- **Higher-risk elements:** reproducing **logos and signage** precisely (the Santa Monica Pier arch sign is reportedly a city trademark the city licenses for commercial use; Pacific Park licenses its own imagery); implying sponsorship or endorsement; using a landmark name or logo in the **channel name, thumbnails as branding, or merchandise**; and a few landmarks with special rights (the Hollywood Sign is licensed and trademarked; the Eiffel Tower night lighting is copyrighted in France). Pier sign trademark: VERIFIED (secondary: Santa Monica Daily Press quoting a city attorney), https://www.smdp.com/city-hall-caught-in-sharknado/ . Hollywood Sign permits page: https://hollywoodsign.org/permits-filming
- **Mitigation:** stylize or alter signage text, don't put landmark logos in branding or thumbnails, and add no "official" language. Filming permits apply only to physical filming, not to a CG rendition.

## 5. The playbook's 7 mistakes checked against official policy

| # | Playbook claim | Verdict | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Uploading the same video twice | **Partially supported** (strong on IG, YT and FB; indirect on TikTok CRP) | Instagram is officially less likely to recommend reels already on Instagram. Facebook reduces distribution of detected duplicates. YouTube Spam policy targets high volumes of near-identical content, though "a few variations" are OK. TikTok's CRP FAQ doesn't name self-reuploads; TikTok Shop guidance says not to re-upload the same video. Cross-posting the same video to *different* platforms is not prohibited anywhere I found; just remove the other platform's watermark. |
| 2 | Copying another creator's script | **Confirmed in principle** | All three platforms treat content copied from others as unoriginal: TikTok Originality Policy, YouTube reused/inauthentic content, Meta unoriginal content. | 3 | Reusing the same set, opening or ending | **Partially supported** | A consistent branded intro, outro or set is normal series branding; no policy bans it. The risk is when **whole videos** are templated: YouTube inauthentic content ("templated storylines", "generic templates giving the impression of mass production") and the Spam example (same music plus repetitive AI imagery across many videos). TikTok Shop warns about near-identical AI template output. Vary the story, camera and action enough that each video is clearly distinct. |
| 4 | Popular free music | **Partially supported** | TikTok: General Library music is not cleared for commercial or business use; the CML is. No official rule found that it disqualifies organic CRP videos. YouTube: any licensed track halves (or more) a Short's pool allocation; Shorts over 1 minute with a Content ID claim are blocked; "free" tracks may be in Content ID. The YouTube Audio Library is claim-free. Best option: original or owned music (or no-claim libraries). |
| 5 | Videos under 61 seconds don't earn | **Confirmed for TikTok CRP only** | TikTok requires "at least one minute" / "over a minute", so 61 seconds or more is a sensible buffer. **Not true for YouTube Shorts** (Shorts of any length up to 3 minutes share pool revenue by engaged views) or for Facebook. |
| 6 | Accounts that look like duplicates or impersonation | **Confirmed** | TikTok prohibits impersonation and ban evasion, and CRP excludes metrics from connected accounts. YouTube has an impersonation policy and extends enforcement across a person's channels. Meta removed more than 20M impersonating accounts in 2025. Multiple accounts are allowed if they are not deceptive. Use one clearly branded account per platform with a unique name, avatar and bio. |
| 7 | Judging or deleting too early; deleting during an appeal loses it | **Partially supported** | Officially, deleted or private TikTok videos are excluded from CRP and their rewards are not paid (Creator Academy), so deleting forfeits earnings, including any reinstated by a successful appeal. No official text says deletion voids the appeal itself; that part is a creator anecdote. "Don't judge too early" (videos picking up later) is a creator anecdote, not policy. |

### Extra: the playbook's "five non-original or low-quality flags in 30 days removes you from CRP"

**Mostly confirmed, with corrections.** Officially: "5 or more **ineligible** videos in the past 30 days" means the account "**may** be disqualified". The trigger counts any reason a video is ineligible, and removal is discretionary rather than automatic. Appeal account disqualification within 30 days.

### Automation, bulk posting and multi-account notes

- Official APIs for posting exist on both TikTok (Content Posting API, audited for public posting) and YouTube (Data API, audit recommended). Using them is allowed. What policies target is **mass-produced, near-identical content**, not the posting method.
- Meta, YouTube and TikTok all police **networks of accounts** posting the same content (YouTube Spam "coordinated networks of channels", Meta duplicate reduction, TikTok "accounts acting in connection"). Do **not** run multiple same-platform accounts posting the same videos.
- The same video posted once to each of TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook is fine. Upload clean masters without watermarks.
