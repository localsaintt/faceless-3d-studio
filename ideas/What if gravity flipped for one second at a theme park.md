# What if gravity flipped for one second at a theme park

**Hook (first 2 seconds):** A roller coaster train lifts off the track.

**Escalation ladder:**
1. Churros and hats rain upward
2. Everyone floats one second up
3. Then falls one second down
4. The fountain shoots ten times higher

**Ending / twist:** Everyone lands, except one balloon.

**Comment prompt:** Would you rather be on a ride or in line?
