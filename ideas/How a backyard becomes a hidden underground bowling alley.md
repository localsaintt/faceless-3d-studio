**Hook (first 2 seconds):** A lawn hatch opens to a glowing staircase.

**Escalation ladder:**
1. Excavation
2. Concrete shell and drainage
3. Maple lanes go in
4. Pin-setter machines
5. Neon and the first strike

**Ending / twist:** The lawn closes and nobody would ever know.
