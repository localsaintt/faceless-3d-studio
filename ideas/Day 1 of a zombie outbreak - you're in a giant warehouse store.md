# Day 1 of a zombie outbreak: you're in a giant warehouse store

**Hook (first 2 seconds):** The doors lock with you inside.

**Escalation ladder:**
1. Pick a base: tents, top shelves, or the loading dock
2. Water vs canned food
3. Forklift barricade
4. The skylight is the only exit

**Ending / twist:** Day 2 starts with a knock on the roof.

**Comment prompt:** Which aisle would you hide in?
