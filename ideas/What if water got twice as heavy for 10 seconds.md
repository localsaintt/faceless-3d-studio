# What if water got twice as heavy for 10 seconds

**Hook (first 2 seconds):** The wave pool knocks everyone flat in the first second.

**Escalation ladder:**
1. Pool floats sink
2. Water slides slow to a crawl, then surge
3. Splashes land like hail
4. Lazy river turns into a conveyor belt

**Ending / twist:** When it snaps back, every pool launches a wave at once.

**Comment prompt:** What would you grab first?
