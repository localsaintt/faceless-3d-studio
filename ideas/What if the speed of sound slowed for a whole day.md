# What if the speed of sound slowed for a whole day

**Hook (first 2 seconds):** Dawn: alarm clocks ring, and nobody wakes up on time.

**Escalation ladder:**
1. Morning traffic: a city of sonic booms
2. School bells arrive after recess
3. Concerts out of sync
4. Thunderstorm with silent lightning
5. Midnight: everything arrives at once

**Ending / twist:** The ice-cream truck song finally arrives, a day late.

**Comment prompt:** Which hour would be worst?
