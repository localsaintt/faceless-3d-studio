# What if sand stopped being solid for 5 seconds

**Hook (first 2 seconds):** Umbrellas sink straight down.

**Escalation ladder:**
1. Sandcastles melt into puddles
2. People sink to their knees
3. Waves of sand roll down the dunes
4. A lifeguard tower tilts

**Ending / twist:** When it turns solid again, everyone is stuck where they stood.

**Comment prompt:** How deep would you sink?
