# What if shadows became solid for 10 seconds

**Hook (first 2 seconds):** A kid steps onto a lamp post's shadow and starts climbing.

**Escalation ladder:**
1. People trip over each other's shadows
2. Pigeons land on shadows mid-air
3. A bus shadow traps a whole crosswalk
4. The sun moves and the shadows start sliding

**Ending / twist:** A cloud passes and everyone standing on shadows drops.

**Comment prompt:** Whose shadow would you climb?
